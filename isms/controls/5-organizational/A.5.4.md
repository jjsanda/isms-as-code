# A.5.4 — Management responsibilities

## Objective

Ensure that managers at every level actively require their people to apply the policies and give them the means to do so, and that top management visibly leads the ISMS rather than delegating it away.

## Implementation

- POL-ISMS ISMS-6 and ISMS-7 state the accountability of the Managing Director and the duty of managers; POL-ORG ORG-1, ORG-2 and ORG-6 detail it.
- The management body is trained annually (POL-HR HR-6) and reviews the ISMS under PROC-MR; the Security Steering Committee tracks resources and decisions every quarter.
- Policy breaches carry consequences under POL-ISMS ISMS-10 and POL-HR HR-10, applied by line managers.

## Evidence

- Management review minutes with decisions and resource allocations — Ticket system, project ISMS-MR.
- Management-body and manager training records — Training platform.
- Managers' confirmation of policy communication in their area — Steering committee pack.

## Metrics

- Management review held with all required inputs (target 1 per year)
- Managers with completed annual responsibility training (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Management review as a formality — PROC-MR fixes inputs, outputs and deadlines for decisions.
- Managers assuming security is the ISMS Manager's job — ORG-6 makes compliance in their area their responsibility, measured through training completion and reporting behaviour.
