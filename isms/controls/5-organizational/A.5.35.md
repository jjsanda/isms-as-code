# A.5.35 — Independent review of information security

## Objective

Have the ISMS examined by people who did not build or run it — internal auditors, external reviewers, the certification body — so that weaknesses are found before an incident or a customer finds them.

## Implementation

- POL-ISMS ISMS-11 mandates annual internal audits; PROC-AUDIT stages 1 to 8 define the programme, independence, conduct, findings, reporting, follow-up and the three-yearly external review.
- Findings flow into PROC-CAPA and PROC-MR.

## Evidence

- Three-year audit programme and annual plans — ISMS records area; management review minutes.
- Audit reports — Ticket system, project ISMS-AUDIT.
- External review reports — ISMS records area.

## Metrics

- Planned audits performed in the year (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- The ISMS Manager auditing the ISMS — the Internal Audit Lead and cross-entity auditors keep independence.
- Findings without follow-up — PROC-CAPA deadlines and effectiveness checks.
