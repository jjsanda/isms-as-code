# A.5.6 — Contact with special interest groups

## Objective

Keep PaketPort connected to the people who see threats first — the national CSIRT, the postal and logistics community and the suppliers' security channels — so that intelligence reaches detection, patching and awareness quickly.

## Implementation

- POL-ORG ORG-11 names the memberships and the two-working-day hand-off into threat intelligence (POL-IR IR-15) and vulnerability management (POL-VULN VULN-2).
- The Security Engineer runs the weekly triage; sanitised lessons are shared back with the community and carriers (IR-15).

## Evidence

- List of memberships and advisory subscriptions — Ticket system, project ISMS-CONTACTS.
- Intelligence intake records with decisions and actions — Threat intelligence log under POL-IR.
- Conference and peer-exchange attendance — Training records.

## Metrics

- Relevant advisories turned into actions within two working days (target ≥ 90 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Advisories read but not acted on — the intake log records a decision for every relevant item.
- Memberships lapsing with a leaver — they are role-based and listed in the contacts project.
