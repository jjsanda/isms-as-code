# A.5.20 — Addressing information security within supplier agreements

## Objective

Put PaketPort's security expectations — confidentiality, incident notification, audit rights, sub-processing, deletion, bills of materials, vetting — into every contract that matters, so that they are enforceable rather than hoped for.

## Implementation

- POL-SUP SUP-3 lists the annex clauses, SUP-4 the carrier-specific additions (API security, availability, mutual notification per LEG-05) and SUP-14 the outsourced-development terms.
- The Head of Procurement owns the template; the Data Protection Officer adds the data-processing terms (POL-LEG LEG-11).

## Evidence

- Security annex template and signed annexes per tier 1 and tier 2 supplier — Contract files; supplier register.
- Carrier agreements with the information security annex — Contract files.

## Metrics

- Tier 1 and tier 2 contracts with the current security annex (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Contracts signed before the annex is negotiated — SUP-2 and the exception process make this visible and time-limited.
- Audit rights never exercised — PROC-AUDIT schedules supplier audits in the programme.
