# A.5.10 — Acceptable use of information and other associated assets

## Objective

Make the everyday rules — what you may do with PaketPort information, devices, email and web access — explicit, acknowledged and enforced, so that technical controls are not undone by habit.

## Implementation

- POL-AUP AUP-1 to AUP-17 set the rules for information, devices, clear desk, remote work, software, web access, email and messaging, monitoring and consequences.
- Acknowledgement is part of onboarding and of the annual training (POL-HR HR-5); enforcement runs through endpoint management, the software catalogue, the web filter and the mail gateway; violations follow POL-HR HR-10.

## Evidence

- Acknowledgements of POL-AUP at onboarding and in the annual training — Training platform.
- Endpoint compliance and web-filter reports — Endpoint management; web filter console.

## Metrics

- Active users with a current POL-AUP acknowledgement (target ≥ 98 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Rules written for office staff only — field technicians have their own clauses for tablets, keys and vehicles.
- Acknowledgement once at hiring — annual re-acknowledgement in the training keeps it current.
