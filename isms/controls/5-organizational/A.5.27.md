# A.5.27 — Learning from information security incidents

## Objective

Make every significant incident leave PaketPort stronger — a fixed root cause, an updated risk, a better playbook, a lesson told to staff in all entities.

## Implementation

- POL-IR IR-13 (review within ten working days, actions in PROC-CAPA, risk updates under PROC-RISK, sharing) and IR-14 (statistics to the steering committee).
- PROC-IR stage 8 closes incidents only after the review; PROC-CAPA tracks actions to effectiveness. Partially implemented: cross-entity lessons sessions are planned for the next cycle.

## Evidence

- Post-incident review reports with actions — Ticket system, project INC.
- Corrective actions from incidents — Ticket system, project ISMS-CAPA.
- Lessons published in the monthly awareness communication — Intranet archive.

## Metrics

- S1 and S2 incidents with a post-incident review within ten working days (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Reviews that blame people — POL-IR IR-6 and POL-HR HR-10 protect good-faith reporters, and reviews look at causes.
- Actions agreed but never verified — PROC-CAPA step 6 checks effectiveness.
