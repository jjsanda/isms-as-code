# A.6.4 — Disciplinary process

## Objective

Respond to policy breaches consistently and fairly — graded, documented, lawful — so that consequences are predictable and reporting stays safe.

## Implementation

- POL-HR HR-10: graded process, works-council involvement in Germany, ISMS Manager consulted on the facts, good-faith reporting protected; POL-ISMS ISMS-10 states the principle and POL-AUP AUP-17 links breaches to it.

## Evidence

- Documented disciplinary process and the works-council agreement (Germany) — HR records.
- Case log (anonymised statistics) — HR system (restricted).

## Metrics

- Cases handled within the documented process with ISMS Manager consultation (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Inconsistent handling between entities — one process with local legal adaptation.
- Punishing the reporter of an incident — POL-IR IR-6 and HR-10 forbid it.
