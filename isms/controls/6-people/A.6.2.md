# A.6.2 — Terms and conditions of employment

## Objective

Make information security part of the deal: employees, contractors and suppliers' staff know their responsibilities, the consequences and their confidentiality duty from the first day.

## Implementation

- POL-HR HR-3 (contract contents) and HR-4 (biennial review of the agreements); POL-AUP is acknowledged at onboarding (HR-5); contractual consequences for suppliers under POL-SUP SUP-3.

## Evidence

- Contract templates with security clauses and their legal review — HR templates; legal review records.
- Signed contracts and confidentiality agreements — HR system; supplier files.

## Metrics

- Active staff and contractors with signed security and confidentiality clauses (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Clauses copied years ago — HR-4 reviews them every two years with the DPO and counsel.
- Contractors without the same duties — HR-3 covers contractors and visitors too.
