# A.6.5 — Responsibilities after termination or change of employment

## Objective

Make sure people who leave or change role know which duties continue — confidentiality, returning information — and that their knowledge and assets stay with PaketPort.

## Implementation

- POL-HR HR-11 and HR-12: mover and leaver triggers, the written reminder, the handover; PROC-JML stage 3.4 (in draft) details the exit steps; asset return under POL-CLS CLS-4.

## Evidence

- Exit conversation records with the written reminder — HR system.
- Handover checklists for critical roles — Ticket system, queue ACCESS.

## Metrics

- Leavers with a recorded exit conversation and reminder (target 100 %)

## Common pitfalls

- Departures handled by IT alone — HR, IT, Facilities and the line manager each have steps.
- Knowledge leaving with the person — handover before the last day and runbooks under A.5.37.
