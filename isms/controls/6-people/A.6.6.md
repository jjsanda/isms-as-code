# A.6.6 — Confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements

## Objective

Bind everyone who sees PaketPort's or its partners' confidential information to keep it confidential — during and after the relationship.

## Implementation

- POL-HR HR-3 (clause in contracts; agreements for contractors, suppliers' staff and visitors) and HR-4 (biennial review); POL-PHY PHY-4 requires it for visitors to restricted zones; POL-SUP SUP-3 for suppliers.

## Evidence

- Confidentiality agreement templates and review records — HR templates; legal review.
- Signed agreements of contractors and of visitors to secure areas — HR system; visitor management.

## Metrics

- External persons with access and without a signed agreement (target 0)

## Common pitfalls

- Agreements that stop at termination — HR-3 requires survival.
- Visitors in restricted zones without one — visitor management checks before entry.
