# A.7.5 — Protecting against physical and environmental threats

## Objective

Protect equipment and sites against fire, water, power problems, weather, vehicle impact and vandalism — from the server room to the street corner.

## Implementation

- POL-PHY PHY-9 (server rooms and depot protection, battery safety in the lab) and PHY-10 (site survey, enclosure ratings, anchoring); utilities under PHY-15 and PHY-16; continuity under POL-BC.

## Evidence

- Fire, water and environmental protection inspection records — Facilities records.
- Site survey reports for locker sites — Fleet-management plane; installation records.

## Metrics

- Locker sites installed without a site survey (target 0)

## Common pitfalls

- Sites chosen by sales alone — the survey includes flood, impact and lighting criteria.
- Fire protection in the lab sized for an office — battery-powered test units need suitable protection.
