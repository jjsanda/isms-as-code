# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming and
inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive
criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and publishing
others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to
**<pepek.svanda@gmail.com>**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
